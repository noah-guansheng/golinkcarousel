Last updated: May 26, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how golinkcarousel.com ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our LinkedIn Carousel PDF Generator website.

The data controller responsible for your personal data under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and other applicable privacy laws is the operator of golinkcarousel.com. For privacy-related inquiries, please contact us at privacy@golinkcarousel.com.

Our tool processes images locally in your browser to generate PDF files. We do not require account registration, and uploaded images are not transmitted to our servers unless explicitly stated otherwise in a future feature.

When you visit our website, we and our third-party partners may automatically collect certain information, including:

  • IP address and approximate geographic location

Where GDPR applies, we process personal data on the following bases:

  • Consent — for non-essential cookies and personalized advertising, where required by law.
  • Legitimate interests — to operate, secure, and improve our website, prevent fraud, and analyze aggregated usage.
  • Legal obligation — where we must comply with applicable laws or regulatory requests.

We use cookies and similar technologies to operate our website, remember preferences, measure performance, and display advertisements.

Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to our website or other websites on the Internet. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to golinkcarousel.com and/or other sites on the Internet.

You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting:

Where required by law (including in the EU/EEA and UK), we will request your consent before placing non-essential cookies. You can manage or withdraw consent at any time through your browser settings or our cookie preferences, if available.
